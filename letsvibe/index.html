Your path from curious to builder

    Learn the fundamentals

    Work through Modules 1–3 of the curriculum: how AI works, the vibe coding mindset, and the tool landscape. Watch one Fundamentals video alongside each module.

  2. STAGE 2

    Follow guided tutorials

    Choose your track →
  3. STAGE 3

    Build the three labs
